# SideQuest design context

Stated by Cyn (design owner) across the design reviews of November 2026. Read this before any design work.

## Who it is for
Santa Clara University students with a gap between commitments, on a phone, usually walking or between classes.
Hosts (clubs, departments) who post events.

## Jobs
1. "I'm free from 1:10 to 1:55. What can I attend in full, and where is it?"
2. Plan it, see my day, notice a clash, fix it.
3. Ask a group: who can come? (Works / If… / Can't)
4. Walk there on the campus map.
5. Post an event in under a minute.

## How it should feel
A paper travel desk for campus: tickets, postmarks, a dotted trail. Warm, a little witty, never corporate.
Calm and plain-spoken. It should not look AI-made.

## The rule behind every choice
Paper stays quiet and ink does the talking. One loud thing per screen, and that thing is the event.

## Materials, one job each
- Ticket (time on a stub, dotted line, two half-circle bites, apothecary outline): an event. The same ticket on every page.
- Brush stroke: the one main action on a screen (Plan it, Post event, Send, I'm in, Make it a plan). Never more than one in view where avoidable.
- Washi strip: a filter tag. Nothing else.
- Gold: free time and today. Today on the timeline is a flat gold bead with a maroon star.
- Dotted line: the trail joining days, and a walking route.
- Postcard stamp ("Greetings from"): the event's picture until a host adds one. Maroon glass, gold hairline, cream serif.
- Plain maroon ink text: every secondary action and every link. An arrow only when it leads to another page.

## Type
Only Instrument Serif (titles and times, with a slight stroke for weight) and Urbanist. The allowed sizes live in
`app/e2e-demo/type-scale.json`; new text picks one of them.

## Colour
Maroon and gold on warm cream. Category colours are controlled and appear as small marks, never as large fills.
Red ink is the one warning colour (clashes). No pure white or pure black.

## Never
- Pill or capsule buttons, glowing shapes, gradient blobs, 3D shading on the timeline.
- Tilted labels or boxes used as decoration. Labels sit flat.
- Text cut off with "…", or anything crossing a card's lines. Cards grow taller instead.
- The same fact twice on one screen (dates, weekdays, the year).
- A big number with a small label as a page header.
- A two-line, two-colour headline in the top-left corner.
- Links that leave SideQuest when an in-app screen can do it.
- Emoji as icons.

## Privacy stance, visible in the design
No live location is shared. Friends going shows only inside groups and only when you opt in. Accounts are scu.edu only.

## Words
One name per thing: free time, busy time, My Schedule, plan, clash, Someday, group, host, RSVP, Works / If… / Can't.
Few words. No em dashes or semicolons in interface text.

## Checks
`npm run test:ux` runs the design suite: type scale, naming, card fit, polish (nothing cut, crossed, jammed or split),
conventions (tap targets, brush only on buttons). A design change updates these tests in the same change.
