## Approved MVP scope evolution and remaining documentation needs

**The original team [P3 MVP requirements](https://docs.google.com/document/d/11Yq8sW1aOphznZHP-zbul2OedCbLJtxczb-ZZDD3oR8/edit) describe a broader MVP than the later [P4-based implementation playbook](MVP_TEAM_PLAYBOOK.md).** The team reports that Professor Kandel reviewed the reasoning and **approved the narrower scope**. This is an approved scope evolution, not an open instructor-approval problem. The original P3 includes:
- Official and student-posted event browsing and the ability to distinguish their source.
- **Partial as well as full** attendance, explanations for fit, usable time, and campus travel time.
- Event cost, registration, attendance flexibility, and event availability.
- Event sharing and sufficient details for others to participate.

The later P4-based scope chooses **student posting → shared catalog browsing → full-fit search → current-device planned-event IDs**. **Instructor approval was confirmed by the team in conversation.** The remaining improvement is to link or summarize that decision in the repository's current requirements and clearly mark which earlier P3 features are postponed. This audit does not independently possess a separate written approval record.

**Documentation update recommended:** make the professor-approved narrower scope explicit in the current MVP requirements/specifications, with a deferred-features table. No new approval is presumed necessary from the information supplied. Keep the M1 walking-skeleton scope separate: [M1 requirements](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1yTrAU-RBO4YYBJLUYtjB1kb0U94_vN3iIjDeQBZ-gk0/edit) deliberately use browser-local, full-fit-only events.
