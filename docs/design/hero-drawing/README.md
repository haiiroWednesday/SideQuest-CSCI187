# Discover hero drawing

`app/src/view/assets/mission-arcade-trail.svg` is generated by these two scripts.

```
python3 docs/design/hero-drawing/scene.py app/src/view/assets/mission-arcade-trail.svg
```

`pen.py` draws every line as a tapered, slightly wobbly ink stroke. `scene.py` lays out the mission arcade, the bell tower and three Mexican fan palms (Washingtonia robusta) like the ones on campus: ringed trunks that flare at the base, a short skirt of dry leaves, and fan leaves whose segments split and hang at the tips. The red dashed trail and the X are kept from the first version. The random seed is fixed, so the output is the same every run.

## Spot drawings

`graphics.py` uses the same pen for twelve spot drawings (empty state, claw machine, bike, categories, market, bell, club stamps). They sit in `graphics/` and are not wired into the app yet.

```
python3 docs/design/hero-drawing/graphics.py docs/design/hero-drawing/graphics
```

## Mission Gardens, the church, and flower studies

Ink only, no colour. `flowers.py` draws the flowers from real petal shapes: roses with a spiral heart and petals that roll back at the rim, wax begonias with two round and two narrow tepals, and wisteria clusters of pea-shaped florets. `garden.py` is the rose walk under the wisteria pergola, `church.py` is the Mission church seen from the fountain, and `study.py` shows the flowers large. The SVGs are heavy (5 to 10 MB), so `garden/` keeps 3x WebP renders.

```
python3 docs/design/hero-drawing/garden.py garden.svg
python3 docs/design/hero-drawing/church.py church.svg
```

## Flat outlined object scenes

The style from Cynthia's reference (one even outline, flat fills, a soft circle behind, bursts, dots, a looping line), with objects only. `flat4.py` draws seven: free time in a jar, Saturday opening like a door, the hourglass garden, a paper boat on the week, the clock snail, an island in a teacup, and the invite. Output is in `flat/`.

```
python3 docs/design/hero-drawing/flat4.py docs/design/hero-drawing/flat
```
